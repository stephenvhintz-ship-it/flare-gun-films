About

Flare Gun Films

Flare Gun Films is an independent production company founded by writer-producer Stephen Hintz. The company develops character-driven features from true stories, literary material, and imagined worlds—projects with a clear point of view and something real at stake.

Before he wrote screenplays, Stephen ran a social service agency in Milwaukee, working with kids and families. That’s still the material: what people inherit, what they lose, and what they’ll do to get it back.

He co-wrote The Hill (Dennis Quaid, Briarcliff Entertainment, 2023), executive produced Battlecreek (dir. Alison Eastwood), and co-wrote The Forever Tree, a short directed by Alrick Brown that premiered at the Bentonville Film Festival.
